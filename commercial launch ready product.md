## First Outcome: Diagram Mapper — Commercial Launch-Ready Product

The desired artifact is a fully implemented, tested, documented, and deployable version of Diagram Mapper, based on the existing repository:

https://github.com/pve-homelab/Diagram-Mapper

The application must be ready to launch as a commercial online service with a subscription business model.

### Required Deliverables

1. **Working application:** A complete implementation of the agreed product requirements, including infrastructure repository analysis, accurate interactive architecture diagrams, resource exploration, contextual AI explanations, and the associated user interface.

2. **Professional UI/UX:** A polished, consistent, accessible interface with intuitive navigation, onboarding, and an excellent user experience.

3. **Verified quality:** Automated and manual testing, documented results, resolved release-blocking defects, security checks, and evidence that critical features work correctly.

4. **Commercial readiness:** A validated target market, competitor research, recommended pricing, subscription and billing integration, user account management, and a clear product positioning strategy.

5. **Production deployment:** A working deployment in the intended hosting environment, with appropriate configuration, monitoring, backups, security controls, and operational documentation.

6. **Customer-facing materials:** A professional landing page, product documentation, onboarding instructions, and initial marketing materials.

7. **Complete project documentation:** Architecture diagrams, technical decisions, setup instructions, deployment and recovery procedures, test reports, known limitations, operating costs, and a prioritised post-launch roadmap.

8. **Verified handover:** A final release with versioned source code in GitHub, reproducible build and deployment instructions, passing relevant tests, and evidence that the agreed acceptance criteria have been met.

### Definition of Done

The outcome is complete when Diagram Mapper can be deployed and operated as a real product, customers can successfully use its core functionality, and the infrastructure required to offer and manage paid subscriptions is operational.

All critical functionality must be independently verified. Security, licensing, privacy, billing, and operational requirements must be addressed, and no unresolved release-blocking defects may remain.

Any outstanding limitations or risks must be explicitly documented.

### Success Criteria

I should have a working application that I can demonstrate to prospective customers, make available to paying subscribers, and continue maintaining and improving.

The deliverable is the actual software product and its supporting commercial infrastructure—not merely a proposal, prototype, mock-up, backlog, or report.

Begin with an evidence-based audit of the existing repository and market opportunity, then execute the work through measurable milestones until the launch criteria are satisfied.
