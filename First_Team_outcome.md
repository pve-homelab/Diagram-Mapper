Objective: Turn Diagram Mapper into a commercially viable, production-ready SaaS product.
Take the existing Diagram Mapper GitHub repository from its current state through to a fully completed, tested, polished, and deployable application that is ready to be offered to paying customers through an online subscription service.
The team is responsible for the entire product lifecycle, not just software development. Begin by auditing the existing codebase, identifying incomplete features, technical debt, bugs, and architectural limitations. Conduct market research and competitor analysis to understand the target audience, validate demand, identify differentiating features, and develop a commercially realistic product and pricing strategy.
Plan and execute the development roadmap, covering application architecture, backend and frontend development, feature completion, database design, security, performance, scalability, and reliability. Make informed UI/UX decisions based on usability, accessibility, modern design standards, and the expectations of the intended customers. The finished application should feel like a cohesive, professional product rather than a technical prototype.
Establish comprehensive automated and manual testing, including unit, integration, end-to-end, security, performance, and usability testing as appropriate. Verify that features work correctly, resolve defects, document the results, and ensure that releases meet clearly defined acceptance criteria.
In parallel, prepare the business for launch: subscription and billing integration, account management, onboarding, deployment infrastructure, monitoring, backups, documentation, customer support processes, landing page, product messaging, and launch marketing. Identify any legal, privacy, licensing, or operational requirements that must be addressed before commercial release.
Organise the work into clearly owned tasks with dependencies, milestones, deliverables, and measurable definitions of done. Use specialist agents for engineering, product management, market research, UI/UX design, QA, security, DevOps, and marketing where appropriate. Agents should collaborate, review one another's work, document decisions, and independently verify completed deliverables rather than simply reporting success.
Definition of success: Diagram Mapper is a fully functional, professionally designed, secure, documented, tested, and deployable product with a validated commercial direction and the infrastructure required to launch and operate it as a paid online service. All critical functionality must be verified, release-blocking defects resolved, and outstanding risks explicitly documented. The final deliverable must be ready for real customers, not merely demonstrate a working prototype.
Work autonomously wherever possible, escalating significant architectural, financial, commercial, or irreversible decisions for my approval. Prioritise delivering a genuinely useful, maintainable product that customers will pay for over adding features for their own sake.
